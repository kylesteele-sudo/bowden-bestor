Direction at a Glance

The Zoom discussion established a clear direction: a reliable, low-maintenance home with intuitive controls, approachable for the Primaries and guests with minimal instruction.

This post-meeting submittal records the direction, open decisions, and coordination items for the Primaries and project team as Global Wave Integration develops the design, proposal, and installation plan.

Simple, reliable, approachable technology. Cost-effective by design.
Crestron Home brings technology under one umbrella. Independent subsystems and native apps provide alternate control paths when one system fails.
01

Control

Simple, connected control.
Confirmed Direction

Crestron Home is the home-control platform for the design, bringing integrated technology together through one familiar interface.

Subsystems will retain independent operation, with native apps for all selected services on the iPads providing alternate control paths when one system fails. Sonos, lighting if selected, and simple TV controls will remain accessible through native apps or controls if Crestron Home is unavailable.

The goal is to prevent an issue with one system from taking everything else down.
02

iPads

Flexible control with iPads.
Confirmed Direction

iPads were chosen over dedicated touch panels for cost and ease of use — including the ability to grab an iPad and go. Removable mounting and charging options will support portable use and access to individual service apps.

Proposed locations (preliminary): Main entry wall-docked iPad · Great room or kitchen tabletop iPad with IPORT dock · ADU possible additional wall-docked iPad.

Locations, quantities, mounts, and charging arrangements remain preliminary.
03

Network & Wi-Fi

Reliable coverage, thoughtfully integrated into the home.
In Design

Reliable Wi-Fi coverage throughout indoor and outdoor areas was discussed. The network design will support everyday use and connected systems, with access-point locations developed around coverage needs of the property.

Access points will be hidden wherever practical. Where full concealment is unsuitable, a discreet in-ceiling enclosure will be considered. Wired LAN locations around the home will provide flexibility for future data connections.

Network security will be considered alongside coverage: protected administration, controlled access, appropriate separation of guest and home-technology devices, and supported updates.

Access-point models, quantities, enclosure selections, and wired outlet locations remain to be developed.
04

Audio / Music

Sonos integrated with Crestron Home.
Confirmed Direction

Spotify and Apple Music are the listening priorities. Sonos will be integrated with Crestron Home and will also operate through the native Sonos app.

Coverage areas: Great room, kitchen, loggia, other common areas, appropriate TV rooms, primary bathroom and applicable primary suite/wing areas, and outdoors.

Open question for the Primaries: Is audio desired in the primary bedroom? Primary-bedroom speakers are not assumed.

Speaker appearance — conventional architectural in-ceiling/in-wall or Sonance invisible speakers — has not been finalized. Global Wave Integration will coordinate appearance and placement with Gordon Gibson's design team.

05

Video / Media

Selected TV locations.
Confirmed Direction

TVs are planned for the great room, library, ADU, and loggia. Everyday operation should remain familiar, ideally using an Apple TV-style remote, with compatible functions also available through Crestron Home.

Confirmed by Primaries: No television in the primary bedroom.
06

Intercom & Access

Video intercom at the pedestrian gate.
Confirmed Direction

The video intercom will move from the front door to the pedestrian gate and will include access control for housekeepers, pool service, gardeners, guests, and other authorized visitors.

Access methods, permissions, and equipment will be developed during design, including how authorized visitors enter and how access can be managed or revoked.

07

Security Cameras

Camera coverage around the property.
In Design

Maintain planned security camera coverage of the front, rear, and sides of the property. Global Wave Integration will develop preliminary camera locations consistent with the discussion.

Camera models and final counts have not been selected. Camera viewing within Crestron Home will be confirmed against the selected cameras and supported integration features.

08

Alarm

Pre-wire only.
Pre-Wire Only

The alarm scope includes pre-wire only. No alarm panels, detectors, keypads, or other alarm hardware are included. This distinction reflects the approach discussed with the Primaries and Gordon Gibson's team and will be maintained as the design develops.

09

Lighting

Shaped around daily life.
Direction Pending

Lighting control remains an open design decision. No lighting control system, wiring approach, or final lighting plan has been selected or authorized.

Option A — Conventional switches and wireless dimmers: Traditional line-voltage wall switches and dimmers keep control at familiar switch locations.

Option B — Centralized lighting and low-voltage keypads: Centralized lighting control with low-voltage architectural keypads. Greater flexibility in keypad appearance and room design, with finishes ranging from metal to glass to stone.

Ketra (optional): Lutron Ketra can introduce dynamic color temperature and natural light-inspired transitions throughout the day — circadian-oriented lighting that follows the rhythm of daily life.

Awaiting Primaries' direction. Lighting control and Ketra are not approved scope.
10

Motorized Shading

A conversation to continue.
Direction Pending

Motorized shading was briefly discussed and is not currently approved scope. Potential benefits include privacy and room darkening, glare and heat-gain management, protection for furnishings and artwork, and convenient scheduled or integrated operation.

The Primaries will discuss shading and advise whether the concept should be developed further.
11

HVAC

Allow for a possible later connection.
Future Consideration

HVAC control is not part of the initial integration. The home-control platform should allow for possible integration later, subject to HVAC equipment and thermostat compatibility.

Global Wave Integration recommends discussing ecobee thermostats with the HVAC contractor, followed by confirmation of the selected equipment and integration path.

12

Clean Power

A stronger foundation.
Under Consideration

A considered power strategy can help protect sensitive electronics and reduce avoidable interruptions across network, control, and AV systems. Surge protection, power conditioning, and battery backup are complementary — not interchangeable — functions.

The Pacific Palisades context includes documented outage history dating to 2008, with LADWP's March 2026 plan calling for expanded undergrounding with a 2031 planning completion target. No equipment is selected or approved.

Recommendation: keep a right-sized power-quality and backup option in view as the proposal develops.

13

Showroom Visit

An opportunity to explore the technology in person.
When Ready

A future showroom visit was briefly discussed as an opportunity to explore relevant technology and architectural options for the residence. The Primaries will reach out when ready to coordinate a visit.
